about me

I am a student graphic designer from Canberra. I make posters, brand identities and illustrations. I am passionate about combining storytelling and visual design.

In 2025 my design for the CMET Light Rail Safety Week campaign won first place and was displayed on Canberra's light rail trams and station billboards. I've since been commissioned by Gang Gang Cafe & Bar for their event posters and I run a small freelance business alongside my hospitality jobs.

I also build interactive work with AI assisted code. My ANU Creative Computing project used p5.js and hand tracking to turn fungal networks into a living neural net, and I built my portfolio site the same way.

I am currently on a gap year before starting a Bachelor of Design at the University of Melbourne in 2027. In October I'll be in Tokyo for six weeks as a design intern at Vegas PR Group, a bilingual PR and marketing agency for music, fashion and art.
